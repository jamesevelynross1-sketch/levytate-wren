How the calculation works—and where the data comes from

Annual hours freed = people × coordination hours per person per week × assumed reduction × working weeks. Capacity equivalent = hours freed × hourly employer cost. This is released staff capacity, not assumed headcount or payroll reduction.

Combined opportunity = equivalent staff capacity value + separately confirmed net training expenditure genuinely avoided. This combines different types of benefit for discussion; it is not a cash-flow forecast, formal return on investment, or proof of savings.

HMRC’s levy rate is 0.5% of NI-able payroll, less the employer’s allocated allowance. This tool does not reproduce monthly declarations or your account. Levy contributions and expiring funds are not savings.

The £24.59 hourly starting point uses ONS median full-time hourly earnings of £19.67 in April 2025 with an illustrative 25% employer-cost uplift. Use actual costs where possible. Staffing, reduction percentages and working weeks are assumptions—not empirical LevyTate performance. Record a baseline and compare it with a pilot before making an investment decision.
