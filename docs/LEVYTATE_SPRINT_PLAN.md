# LevyTate Sprint Plan

Plan date: 16 June 2026  
Scope: Portakabin LevyTate demo as the reference environment for the next product standard.

## Planning Principles

This sprint plan prioritises work using four criteria:

1. Commercial impact
2. Demo impact
3. UX simplicity
4. Ease of implementation

The aim is to turn the current Portakabin Apprenticeship Hub into a 10/10 executive demonstration that explains LevyTate quickly, feels commercially credible, and supports future employer roll-out.

## Sprint Priority Summary

| Sprint | Theme | Priority Ranking | Estimated Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint 1 | Executive Demo Foundation | 1 | Medium |
| Sprint 2 | Commercial Engine | 2 | Medium |
| Sprint 3 | Executive Reporting | 3 | Medium to High |
| Sprint 4 | Future Talent | 4 | High |

## Sprint 1

### Executive Demo Foundation

Focus:

- Navigation simplification
- Workforce Readiness Score
- Ask LevyTate AI positioning
- Dashboard simplification
- Removal of duplicate content

Expected outcome:
Employer understands LevyTate within 60 seconds.

Objective:
Create a cleaner, faster and more self-explanatory first impression so the platform immediately communicates what LevyTate does, why it matters, and how each stakeholder uses it.

User value:

- Employers can grasp the product without a guided explanation.
- Stakeholders see only the sections relevant to them.
- Employees and managers get a simpler path into applications and development decisions.

Commercial value:

- Stronger first demo experience improves conversion confidence.
- Cleaner messaging makes it easier to position LevyTate as a premium operating system rather than a feature collection.
- A clearer AI-first story supports future upsell into provider matching and advisory services.

Features included:

- Simplify sidebar navigation by role and remove overlapping routes
- Make Ask LevyTate AI the primary guided entry point
- Introduce Workforce Readiness as the lead executive signal for leadership views
- Tighten dashboard layouts so each role overview fits comfortably within a single screen
- Remove duplicate pathway discovery and duplicate analytics surfaces
- Reframe dashboard cards with clearer plain-English labels and stronger click targets
- Push secondary tools and admin actions behind expandable sections

Priority ranking:
1

Estimated complexity:
Medium

## Sprint 2

### Commercial Engine

Focus:

- Provider Matching workspace
- Consultancy lead generation
- AI to Provider Matching workflow
- Request Provider Matching journey

Expected outcome:
Platform generates qualified consultancy opportunities.

Objective:
Turn LevyTate's strongest commercial workflows into visible, structured product journeys that capture employer demand and route it into actionable commercial follow-up.

User value:

- Apprenticeship Leads can move from workforce need to provider matching request in one guided flow.
- Employers understand when LevyTate is a platform workflow and when LevyTate is offering expert support.
- Commercial handoff feels deliberate rather than bolted on.

Commercial value:

- Directly supports provider matching opportunities.
- Creates consultancy conversations from workforce planning and role-mapping needs.
- Makes LevyTate's value more tangible than a static dashboard demo.

Features included:

- Add Provider Matching as a top-level Apprenticeship Lead workspace
- Build a visible request journey from AI recommendation to submitted matching request
- Add structured request statuses such as Submitted, Under Review, Shortlist in Progress, and Shortlist Ready
- Capture commercial signals such as learner volume, urgency, preferred site footprint, delivery preference, and advisory need
- Separate Provider Matching from Provider Mappings and Provider Enrolment
- Surface commercial follow-up prompts in AI and reporting where appropriate

Priority ranking:
2

Estimated complexity:
Medium

## Sprint 3

### Executive Reporting

Focus:

- Workforce reports
- Participation reporting
- Skills gap reporting
- Site reporting
- Board-level dashboards

Expected outcome:
Useful for annual planning and board meetings.

Objective:
Make LevyTate credible as an executive planning and reporting platform, not just an approvals workflow.

User value:

- Department Heads and Apprenticeship Leads get concise decision-ready reporting.
- Leaders can compare participation, readiness, learner distribution and future demand by site.
- Reporting becomes useful for planning conversations, not just demo theatre.

Commercial value:

- Strengthens LevyTate's workforce planning proposition.
- Supports strategic advisory and consultancy conversations.
- Creates a stronger case for annual subscriptions and leadership-level usage.

Features included:

- Introduce a board-level executive dashboard anchored on Workforce Readiness
- Consolidate overlapping analytics pages into clearer reporting lenses
- Add participation, readiness and skills-gap trends with year-on-year framing where relevant
- Improve site-level reporting with clearer comparisons and risk signals
- Add executive summaries that connect data to recommended actions
- Standardise reporting data selectors so metrics stay consistent across dashboards and reports

Priority ranking:
3

Estimated complexity:
Medium to High

## Sprint 4

### Future Talent

Focus:

- Future Talent Portal
- School engagement
- Employer challenges
- Talent communities
- Future pipeline scoring

Expected outcome:
Extension of workforce planning beyond current workforce.

Objective:
Extend LevyTate from internal apprenticeship management into future talent pipeline development, while keeping the strategic module clearly separated from the core employer operating system.

User value:

- Employers can show real pathways and early-career opportunities to pupils and schools.
- Schools can track engagement and activity completion.
- Pupils can explore careers, earn badges and receive AI-guided recommendations.

Commercial value:

- Opens school partnership and future talent conversations.
- Extends LevyTate's value beyond current workforce management.
- Creates a higher-value strategic product narrative for employers with long-term pipeline needs.

Features included:

- Move Future Talent fully into the LevyTate app shell as a strategic module
- Create clear employer, school and pupil user journeys
- Add employer brand profiles, pathway maps, challenges and talent community signals
- Add school participation dashboards and engagement analytics
- Add AI-guided career discovery and future pipeline scoring
- Align Future Talent reporting with the same premium executive standard as the Apprenticeship Hub

Priority ranking:
4

Estimated complexity:
High

## Top 10 Quick Wins

1. Rename and reduce overlapping sidebar items across all stakeholder roles.
2. Make Ask LevyTate AI the first visible CTA on the dashboard.
3. Replace generic operating snapshot labels with plain-English business language.
4. Add Workforce Readiness as the first metric for Department Head and Apprenticeship Lead views.
5. Remove duplicate discovery content between Recommended Pathways, Career Pathfinder and Skills Analysis.
6. Tighten dashboard sections so role overviews fit more cleanly above the fold.
7. Move lower-priority provider actions into expandable menus.
8. Add clearer executive subtitles to reports and dashboards.
9. Standardise metric descriptions and click-through actions across summary cards.
10. Clean up any remaining legacy navigation labels that differ from the product specification.

## Top 10 High Impact Changes

1. Reposition Ask LevyTate AI as the hero feature and guided front door to the platform.
2. Establish Workforce Readiness as LevyTate's signature executive metric.
3. Build a first-class Provider Matching workspace for Apprenticeship Leads.
4. Connect AI recommendations to provider matching and advisory workflows.
5. Consolidate reporting into a board-ready executive reporting layer.
6. Unify metrics behind shared selectors so dashboards and detail views always agree.
7. Simplify navigation so each role sees a smaller, clearer operating system.
8. Separate apprenticeship pathways, advisory opportunities and provider workflows more clearly.
9. Improve site-level analytics to support multi-site employers like Portakabin.
10. Position Future Talent as a strategic extension of workforce planning, not an isolated extra feature.

## Top 10 Features To Defer

1. Deep LMS integrations
2. HR system integrations
3. Finance system integrations
4. Single Sign-On
5. Provider relationship management suite
6. Advanced provider comparison tooling
7. Predictive skills forecasting engine
8. Levy optimisation engine
9. Internal career marketplace
10. Complex admin tooling that does not directly improve the employer demo story

## Recommended Delivery Order

1. Complete Sprint 1 before adding more product surface area.
2. Use Sprint 2 to make LevyTate's commercial engine visible and measurable.
3. Use Sprint 3 to strengthen executive credibility and boardroom relevance.
4. Treat Sprint 4 as strategic expansion once the core Apprenticeship Hub story is 10/10.

## Final Recommendation

The next phase should focus on concentration, not expansion. LevyTate already has enough functionality to support a compelling employer demonstration. The highest-value move now is to simplify the experience, make Ask LevyTate AI and Workforce Readiness central, and expose Provider Matching as the clearest commercial journey in the product.
