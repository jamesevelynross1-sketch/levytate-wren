# LevyTate Lift Workforce Capability Assessment — pilot instrument

> Review status: original pilot candidate. Software verification does not make this a scientifically validated, accredited or occupational-competence assessment.

Framework: `lift-workforce-v1`
Questionnaire: `lift-workforce-questionnaire-v1.1`
Interpretation rules: `lift-workforce-rules-v1.1`

The instrument contains three unscored context prompts and 30 substantive prompts across six workplace episodes. It produces evidence states—supported, emerging, insufficient or contradictory—not a score out of 100, pass/fail result or employee ranking.

## Assessment content

### Before you begin

#### intro_responsibilities — Which responsibilities are most relevant to your work at the moment?

Describe the work context only. This is not scored and does not need personal information.

Response: bounded free text. Writing style, spelling and disclosure of personal history are not separately scored.

Evidence type: unscored_context. Unscored context only.

#### intro_interests — What would you most like to get better at or understand more clearly?

Development interests are reflected back to you but never treated as capability evidence.

Response: bounded free text. Writing style, spelling and disclosure of personal history are not separately scored.

Evidence type: unscored_context. Unscored context only.

#### intro_opportunity — Where, if anywhere, could you apply new learning in your current work?

It is fine to say that there is no current opportunity.

Response: bounded free text. Writing style, spelling and disclosure of personal history are not separately scored.

Evidence type: unscored_context. Unscored context only.

### A convincing answer that needs checking

#### check_01 — A recommendation is clear and confident, but one source covers only a small part of the affected work. What is the strongest next step?

Choose the most defensible check before a decision is made.

- limited_pilot. Run a small reversible test now and note that the source is incomplete
- seek_second_source. Find a second source and use agreement between the two as the decision rule
- scope_then_decide. Establish who and what the source covers, identify the material gap, and make the uncertainty explicit before deciding
- defer_for_full_coverage. Delay the decision until a source covering the whole operation is available

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Think clearly; Make information useful.

#### check_02 — An AI assistant drafted the recommendation from supplied notes. Which control matters most before it is used?

Assume the tool is approved, but the decision still matters.

- verify_human_owner. Check material claims against authorised sources, record uncertainty, and keep a named human decision owner
- peer_readability_review. Ask a colleague to review whether the recommendation is clear and proportionate
- model_repeat_check. Run the same prompt again and accept the recommendation if the answer is consistent
- manual_redraft. Rewrite the recommendation manually before using it, without rechecking the underlying claims

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Work intelligently with AI; Protect trust.

#### check_03 — Write two or three sentences explaining the recommendation, the main uncertainty and the next check to a colleague who has not seen the evidence.

Clarity is more important than formal writing style. Do not include confidential information.

Response: bounded free text. Writing style, spelling and disclosure of personal history are not separately scored.

Evidence type: short_work_sample. Capabilities: Make yourself understood; Think clearly.

#### check_04 — The decision is time-sensitive. Which response balances progress and evidence best?

Assume delaying indefinitely also has a cost.

- provisional_full_change. Make the change provisionally, with a review after the first reporting period
- short_evidence_pause. Pause briefly to obtain one additional source, then make the full decision
- partial_implementation. Implement the least disruptive part first and assess whether the remaining change is still needed
- bounded_test. Run a bounded test with an owner, decision date, success measure, and clear stop conditions

Evidence type: interpretation. Capabilities: Turn priorities into progress; Create lasting value.

#### check_05 — When new evidence has challenged your first view, what have you usually done?

Choose the closest answer. Lack of opportunity is recorded as unknown, not weak.

- not_enough_opportunity. I have not had enough opportunity to show this
- current_practice. I tend to retain the first approach unless the evidence is conclusive
- developing_practice. I review the evidence and adjust with some support
- established_practice. I can explain what changed and revise the approach independently

Evidence type: anchored_experience. Capabilities: Grow through change.

### An improvement that moves the problem elsewhere

#### flow_01 — A proposed shortcut reduces one team's handling time but creates extra checking for another team. What should happen next?

Consider the whole flow, not just the first team.

- trial_with_receiver. Run a short trial with the receiving team and compare its experience with the first team's time saving
- map_end_to_end. Map the affected handover and compare total work, rework, risk, and service outcome before choosing
- rebalance_ownership. Proceed if the extra checking can be formally assigned and resourced in the receiving team
- retain_current_flow. Keep the current process because moving work between teams is likely to hide the true cost

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: See better ways; Create lasting value.

#### flow_02 — Which evidence would best test whether the change improved the whole process?

Select the measure that avoids a local-only conclusion.

- team_time_and_feedback. Handling time in both teams plus structured feedback from the people doing the work
- service_outcome_only. Service quality and customer outcome during the test period, compared with the previous period
- end_to_end_evidence. End-to-end elapsed time, rework, service quality, and exceptions for a defined population and comparable period
- first_team_with_exceptions. Time saved by the first team, adjusted for the number of exceptions reported elsewhere

Evidence type: interpretation. Capabilities: Make information useful; Turn priorities into progress.

#### flow_03 — Describe a small test that could reveal downstream consequences before a wider change.

Include a scope, who needs to be involved and one condition for stopping or revising the test.

Response: bounded free text. Writing style, spelling and disclosure of personal history are not separately scored.

Evidence type: short_work_sample. Capabilities: See better ways; Grow through change.

#### flow_04 — The receiving team says the proposal will make their work less reliable. What is the most constructive response?

Choose how to handle evidence and disagreement.

- joint_test_design. Ask for concrete examples, restate the shared outcome, and include the receiving team in the test and stop criteria
- document_and_escalate. Document the concern and ask the process owner to decide whether the efficiency gain outweighs it
- extra_quality_check. Add a temporary quality check for the receiving team while the shortcut is introduced
- revise_before_trial. Revise the proposal using the concern, then return with a complete design before any test

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Help people succeed; Make yourself understood.

#### flow_05 — How do you usually check who could be adversely affected by a process change?

Choose the closest answer. No opportunity is a valid response.

- not_enough_opportunity. I have not had enough opportunity to show this
- current_practice. I focus mainly on the team requesting the change
- developing_practice. I consider affected groups when someone prompts me
- established_practice. I identify affected groups, information risks, and safeguards as part of the change

Evidence type: anchored_experience. Capabilities: Protect trust.

### A team that sees the problem differently

#### team_01 — Two colleagues disagree about the cause of a recurring problem. What is the strongest first move?

The aim is a workable shared understanding, not forced agreement.

- parallel_actions. Let each colleague test the action implied by their explanation and compare what happens
- shared_provisional_cause. Agree a provisional combined explanation so work can continue while more evidence is collected
- facilitated_owner_decision. Ask a neutral process owner to review both explanations and set the next action
- separate_observations. Clarify what each person observed, separate facts from assumptions, and agree evidence that would distinguish the explanations

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Make yourself understood; Help people succeed.

#### team_02 — The examples support more than one explanation. What conclusion is justified?

Choose the statement that preserves uncertainty.

- weighted_explanation. Treat the explanation supported by more examples as the working cause, while recording the alternative
- multiple_plausible. Keep several explanations plausible and gather evidence capable of distinguishing between them before assigning cause
- shared_contribution. Conclude that both causes contribute, because each has at least one supporting example
- operational_not_causal. Avoid a causal conclusion and focus only on an action that could help under either explanation

Evidence type: interpretation. Capabilities: Think clearly; Help people succeed.

#### team_03 — Draft a short message that summarises what is agreed, what remains uncertain and the next commitment.

Use your own words. Writing style and spelling are not scored as separate capabilities.

Response: bounded free text. Writing style, spelling and disclosure of personal history are not separately scored.

Evidence type: short_work_sample. Capabilities: Make yourself understood.

#### team_04 — One colleague holds knowledge needed for the next step but has limited time. What is the best approach?

Balance progress with sustainable knowledge sharing.

- expert_review_point. Prepare the work without them and reserve their limited time for a focused review before commitment
- documented_delegation. Ask them to document the essential knowledge, then allocate the work to someone with capacity
- minimum_contribution. Agree the smallest useful expert contribution, capture it with context, and assign the remaining work and checks clearly
- defer_until_available. Defer the next step until the colleague can participate properly, to avoid avoidable rework

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Help people succeed; Turn priorities into progress.

#### team_05 — When a team commitment is made, how do you usually help it remain useful after the immediate issue?

Choose the closest answer; not enough opportunity is not a negative result.

- not_enough_opportunity. I have not had enough opportunity to show this
- current_practice. I rely mainly on people remembering the agreement
- developing_practice. I sometimes capture an owner or next step
- established_practice. I make ownership, evidence, review, and knowledge transfer explicit

Evidence type: anchored_experience. Capabilities: Create lasting value.

### Numbers that tell an incomplete story

#### data_01 — Reported errors fell from 40 to 30, while completed transactions fell from 1,000 to 500. Which interpretation is defensible?

Use the supplied numbers; no calculation tool is required.

- rate_and_volume. The error count fell, but the observed error rate rose from 4% to 6%; volume and rate moved differently, and cause is not established
- lower_count_better. There were fewer reported errors, so quality improved, although lower volume may have contributed
- rate_worse. The error rate rose by half, so quality declined, even though there were fewer total errors
- not_comparable. The change in volume means the two periods cannot be compared without individual transaction data

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Make information useful; Think clearly.

#### data_02 — Which additional information is most important before comparing two teams' error rates?

Choose the information needed for a fair comparison.

- sample_confidence. Enough cases in each team to know whether the observed difference is stable
- process_conditions. Whether each team uses the same system, training, and quality-control process
- error_impact. The severity and operational impact of the errors, not only their number
- comparable_basis. Comparable definitions, populations, case complexity, and reporting periods, followed by material context such as severity

Evidence type: interpretation. Capabilities: Make information useful.

#### data_03 — Explain the change in errors and transactions without overstating what caused it.

Include the denominator and one limitation.

Response: bounded free text. Writing style, spelling and disclosure of personal history are not separately scored.

Evidence type: short_work_sample. Capabilities: Make yourself understood; Protect trust.

#### data_04 — A leader asks for a single success number. What is the strongest response?

Keep the decision useful while preserving material context.

- headline_with_note. Provide the measure most closely connected to the decision, with a short note on its main limitation
- small_measure_set. Provide a small defined set covering volume, rate, and service outcome, and explain the decision each measure informs
- composite_index. Create a transparent composite index so the leader still receives one number without losing the component definitions
- single_outcome_metric. Use the final service outcome only, because process measures can distract from the result

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Create lasting value; Turn priorities into progress.

#### data_05 — When a measure changes meaning or its data quality is challenged, what do you usually do?

Choose the closest response.

- not_enough_opportunity. I have not had enough opportunity to show this
- current_practice. I tend to keep using the measure for consistency while noting the issue
- developing_practice. I note the issue and seek help to revise the conclusion
- established_practice. I revisit the definition, preserve the previous view, and explain the impact of the change

Evidence type: anchored_experience. Capabilities: Grow through change.

### A new way of working

#### change_01 — You must use a small unfamiliar process tomorrow. What is the strongest preparation?

Assume a short guide and a knowledgeable colleague are available.

- guided_first_case. Arrange for the knowledgeable colleague to guide the first live case and capture any corrections
- low_risk_readthrough. Read the guide, identify a low-risk case, and ask the colleague to confirm the steps before starting
- representative_practice. Practise one representative case, note uncertainties, and confirm escalation and recovery paths before live use
- independent_then_review. Complete the first low-risk case independently and ask the colleague to review the outcome before it is finalised

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Grow through change; Turn priorities into progress.

#### change_02 — The new process includes an automated suggestion. When should the suggestion be accepted?

The automation is intended to assist, not own the decision.

- validated_accountable. When relevant inputs and permissions are valid, material exceptions are checked, and the accountable person agrees
- known_pattern. When the suggestion matches a pattern the team has previously accepted and no obvious exception appears
- confidence_and_review. When the system reports high confidence and a colleague confirms the recommendation is reasonable
- supplier_rules. When it falls within the supplier's stated use case and an audit record is retained

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Work intelligently with AI; Protect trust.

#### change_03 — During practice, a key assumption in the guide proves false. What is the best response?

Choose how to learn without hiding the problem.

- safe_workaround. Use a reversible workaround for the practice case and record it for the process owner
- return_to_old_process. Stop the new process and use the established method until the guide is formally corrected
- complete_then_escalate. Complete the unaffected steps, isolate the failed assumption, and escalate before the affected step
- pause_record_authorise. Pause the affected step, record the mismatch and evidence, seek the right authority, and update the bounded test

Evidence type: interpretation. Capabilities: Grow through change; Think clearly.

#### change_04 — Write a brief handover note explaining the changed assumption and what the next person should do.

Include the boundary of what you know and who can authorise a change.

Response: bounded free text. Writing style, spelling and disclosure of personal history are not separately scored.

Evidence type: short_work_sample. Capabilities: Make yourself understood; Help people succeed.

#### change_05 — How do you usually use feedback after trying a new way of working?

Choose the closest response.

- not_enough_opportunity. I have not had enough opportunity to show this
- current_practice. I mainly use feedback to understand whether people found the change workable
- developing_practice. I make small adjustments when someone identifies an issue
- established_practice. I compare feedback with the intended outcome and revise a bounded part of the process

Evidence type: anchored_experience. Capabilities: See better ways.

### Pressure to deliver

#### pressure_01 — Two urgent requests compete for the same time; one involves confidential information and a fixed external deadline. What should you do first?

Assume you cannot complete both safely without agreement.

- protect_fixed_deadline. Protect the confidential request and fixed deadline first, then notify the other requester of the delay
- risk_priority_escalation. Clarify impact, deadline, dependencies, and confidentiality, then agree a realistic priority or escalate the explicit trade-off
- split_bounded_progress. Make bounded progress on both while asking each requester which minimum outcome they need
- capacity_reassignment. Seek authorised capacity for one request and start the other according to the information currently available

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Turn priorities into progress; Protect trust.

#### pressure_02 — Which evidence best supports a decision about the priority?

Choose evidence connected to outcomes and risk.

- deadline_and_customer. External deadline, customer or service impact, and the consequence of missing each request
- value_and_effort. Expected value, effort, and whether the work can be reversed or recovered later
- outcomes_risk_dependencies. Deadline, affected outcome, harm of delay, dependencies, effort, and information or safety risk
- commitments_and_capacity. Existing commitments, available capacity, and which requester can accommodate a revised date

Evidence type: interpretation. Capabilities: Create lasting value; Make information useful.

#### pressure_03 — Draft a concise escalation that sets out the conflict, your proposed priority and the decision needed.

Do not include real confidential information.

Response: bounded free text. Writing style, spelling and disclosure of personal history are not separately scored.

Evidence type: short_work_sample. Capabilities: Make yourself understood; Help people succeed.

#### pressure_04 — An approved AI tool could speed up part of the urgent confidential task, but its permitted data classification is unclear. What is the strongest action?

Speed does not override permissions.

- confirm_classification. Confirm the permitted data classification and use an authorised alternative until the boundary is clear
- minimum_data_trial. Use only the minimum data needed for a test, remove direct identifiers, and have the output checked manually
- approved_tool_assumption. Use the approved tool for the non-sensitive sections and keep the confidential sections manual
- owner_risk_acceptance. Ask the task owner to accept the information risk if the deadline cannot otherwise be met

Evidence type: structured_judgement. Capabilities: Work intelligently with AI; Protect trust.

#### pressure_05 — After a pressured delivery, how do you usually turn feedback into a better future approach?

Choose the closest response.

- not_enough_opportunity. I have not had enough opportunity to show this
- current_practice. I focus mainly on whether the required deadline was met
- developing_practice. I discuss what could change when a similar issue arises
- established_practice. I capture the outcome, trade-offs, and a specific change with an owner and review point

Evidence type: anchored_experience. Capabilities: Grow through change; Create lasting value.
