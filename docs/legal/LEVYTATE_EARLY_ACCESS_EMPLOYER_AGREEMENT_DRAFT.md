## 3. Early Access scope and authorised users

LevyTate provides invitation-only application, approval, provider-management, role-based and reporting functions during a controlled pilot. The Employer nominates users, keeps membership/roles current and ensures each user uses only their personal account. Features may be limited or changed with reasonable notice where practicable.

## 4. Employer responsibilities and permitted use

The Employer is responsible for the lawfulness, accuracy and minimisation of Employer Data; authorised employment/apprenticeship decisions; user instructions; and keeping its contacts current. Users must not share links or credentials, probe security, access another organisation, upload unlawful or unnecessary sensitive data, reverse engineer beyond applicable rights, or use LevyTate for unlawful discrimination or solely automated employment decisions.

## 5. Data, security and confidentiality

Each party protects confidential information and uses it only for this agreement, subject to standard exclusions and lawful disclosure. LevyTate applies the factual controls in the Security Measures Schedule. The Employer follows access guidance and promptly reports suspected compromise. Data processing is governed by the draft DPA when approved.
