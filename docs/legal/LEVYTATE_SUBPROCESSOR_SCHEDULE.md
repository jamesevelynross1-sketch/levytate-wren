## Authorisation and changes

Proposed agreement position: general written authorisation for the approved list; advance notice of additions/replacements; employer right to object on reasonable data-protection grounds; good-faith alternative; and controlled termination if unresolved. Exact notice periods must reflect supplier notice capability and solicitor advice. LevyTate must subscribe to supplier updates and maintain evidence. OpenAI cannot be added merely because code exists.
