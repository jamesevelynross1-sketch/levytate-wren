# LevyTate Data Processing Agreement — draft

> **Not approved or executed.** Proposed Article 28 UK GDPR schedule to the Employer Agreement. Legal review is mandatory.

## Core terms

1. The Employer is controller and the approved LevyTate operator is processor only for Employer Data described in Annex A. Each remains independently responsible where it acts as controller for its own purposes.
2. LevyTate processes only on documented instructions in the agreement, order form and authorised product use, including transfer instructions, unless law requires otherwise; it informs the Employer where legally permitted. It immediately escalates an instruction it believes infringes applicable law.
3. Authorised personnel are bound by confidentiality. LevyTate implements risk-appropriate measures described in Annex B.
4. The Employer gives **[general/specific—decision required]** written authorisation for Annex C subprocessors. Proposed general-authorisation model includes advance notice, reasonable objection on data-protection grounds and an agreed remediation/exit route. Equivalent obligations flow down and LevyTate remains responsible as required by law.
5. Taking account of processing nature and available information, LevyTate assists with rights requests, security, breach assessment, regulator consultation and DPIAs. Scope/cost treatment requires commercial/legal approval and cannot prevent statutory assistance.
6. At termination and on controller choice, LevyTate returns or deletes Employer Data unless law requires retention, then isolates it. The method, format, window, backup expiry and evidence are Annex E decisions.
7. LevyTate provides information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance and permits audits on proportionate notice and safeguards. Frequency, confidentiality, independent reports, cost and incident exceptions require solicitor approval.
8. Restricted transfers use Annex D only after the parties verify roles, destination, safeguard and transfer risk assessment/data protection test. Supplier terms are not accepted by this draft.
9. LevyTate maintains any processor records required by law. The DPA prevails for a personal-data-processing conflict; mandatory transfer clauses prevail where required.

## Annex A — processing details

- Subject matter: secure hosting and operation of employer apprenticeship applications, approvals, membership/RBAC, learner lifecycle, provider relationships, operational actions and reporting.
- Duration: approved pilot and controlled return/deletion period; exact dates pending.
- Nature: collect, record, organise, store, retrieve, consult, transmit to authorised suppliers/users, restrict, export and delete.
- Purpose: provide employer-instructed LevyTate workflows and support.
- Subjects: employer users, employees/applicants, learners, managers, apprenticeship leads and relevant provider contacts.
- Data: work identity/contact, organisation/role, employment/apprenticeship application, decisions, programme/provider, learner progress/reviews/support, actions, notes and audit context.
- Sensitive data: not intentionally requested; may be entered in free text. Employer must prohibit/minimise unless expressly authorised with a documented lawful basis and Article 9/10 condition.
- Controller obligations: lawful basis, notices, instructions, accuracy, minimisation, authority, retention, rights and decisions.

## Annex B — technical and organisational measures

The factual schedule `LEVYTATE_SECURITY_MEASURES_SCHEDULE.md` is incorporated only after approval; planned controls are not contractual commitments until implemented and agreed.

## Annex C — approved subprocessors

The reviewed schedule `LEVYTATE_SUBPROCESSOR_SCHEDULE.md` applies after supplier contracts, locations and transfers are confirmed. OpenAI is excluded while Production AI is disabled.

## Annex D — transfers

Record exporter/importer, roles, country, data, UK adequacy, UK IDTA/Addendum or other safeguard, transfer risk assessment and supplementary measures. No transfer entry is approved merely by appearing in the assessment.

## Annex E — return and deletion

On verified controller instruction: freeze new processing; provide the approved export; confirm receipt; remove active copies under controlled change; retain only legally required isolated evidence; allow backups to expire under the approved cycle; obtain supplier deletion evidence where available; record completion. Exact format, timing, backup period and exceptions remain decisions.
