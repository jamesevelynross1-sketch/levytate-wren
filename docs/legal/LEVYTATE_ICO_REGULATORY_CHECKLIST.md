## Assessment result

The fee/registration assessment **must be completed before pilot**. Current evidence shows the proposed operator will process personal data electronically and act as an independent controller for security, support and contractual records. The general exemption cannot be assumed. GOV.UK/ICO guidance says controllers normally pay unless exempt and warns that the supplied address appears on the public register. Professional confirmation remains required; do not publish a registration number until verified.
