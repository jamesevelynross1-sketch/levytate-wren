# LevyTate Executive Demo Script

Script date: 16 June 2026  
Purpose: Standard demonstration guide for LevyTate founders, consultants and sales teams.  
Audience: HR Directors, L&D Directors, Apprenticeship Leads, Operations Directors, Managing Directors and executive teams.

## How To Use This Script

This script is designed to help the LevyTate team run a clear, commercially strong employer demonstration. It should be used as a guide, not read word for word. The best demos feel conversational, but the structure, sequence and key messages should stay consistent.

The goal of the demo is simple:

- show the employer problem clearly
- prove that LevyTate solves it in a practical way
- make Ask LevyTate AI, Workforce Readiness and Provider Matching feel strategically valuable
- create momentum toward a pilot, proof of concept or rollout

## Section 1

## Opening Positioning

### 30 Second Introduction

"LevyTate is an AI-guided apprenticeship and workforce readiness platform for employers. It helps employees find the right approved pathways, helps managers and apprenticeship leads manage demand and approvals, and gives leadership a clearer picture of workforce readiness, skills demand and provider planning. In short, it turns apprenticeships from something reactive and fragmented into something strategic and manageable."

### Why Organisations Struggle With Apprenticeships

"Most organisations are not struggling because apprenticeships do not exist. They are struggling because apprenticeship activity is disconnected from the rest of workforce planning. Employees do not know what is relevant. Managers are not always confident approving requests. Apprenticeship leads are often managing demand manually. Leadership cannot easily see what is working, where skills gaps sit, or how provider decisions should be made."

### Why LevyTate Exists

"LevyTate exists to give employers one structured operating system for apprenticeship pathways, approvals, provider support and workforce readiness. It is there to make apprenticeships easier to adopt, easier to manage, and more useful as a business capability tool."

## Section 2

## The Business Problem

### Current State

"For many employers, the current state looks like this:

- apprenticeships are reactive
- managers do not know what is available
- employees do not know what is available
- levy is underutilised
- provider selection is difficult
- workforce planning is disconnected

The result is usually slower decisions, weaker adoption, missed enrolment opportunities and poor visibility for leadership."

### Desired State

"The desired state is very different:

- strategic workforce planning
- clear pathways
- stronger manager engagement
- stronger employee engagement
- better provider decisions

That is the shift LevyTate is designed to create. It takes apprenticeship activity out of email chains, spreadsheets and isolated conversations, and puts it into one role-based system."

## Section 3

## Demo Flow

### 1. Employer Dashboard

What to say:

"I will start with the employer dashboard because this is where LevyTate becomes immediately clear. Instead of a generic admin screen, this gives each stakeholder a role-specific view of what matters to them. For leadership, the emphasis is not just on activity. It is on Workforce Readiness, participation, demand signals, site-level visibility and where action is needed."

"This is important because most employers already have fragmented data. What they do not have is a simple operating picture that connects apprenticeships to organisational capability."

What value it demonstrates:

- executive visibility
- one place for workforce readiness and apprenticeship operations
- clearer decision-making for leadership
- faster understanding of where attention is needed

### 2. Employee Journey

What to say:

"Now I will move into the employee journey. This is where adoption either happens or fails. Employees usually do not need more information. They need clearer guidance. In LevyTate, the employee sees suitable approved pathways, their current application, saved options and development progress without being overwhelmed."

"The platform is designed to reduce confusion. Employees are not browsing a marketplace. They are seeing what is appropriate for their role, their goals and their organisation."

"A key rule here is simplicity. An employee can only have one active apprenticeship application at a time. That keeps the process focused and makes manager review much cleaner."

What value it demonstrates:

- better employee engagement
- less confusion
- stronger adoption of approved pathways
- cleaner application behaviour
- a more manageable process for the employer

### 3. AI Pathway Assistant

What to say:

"This is Ask LevyTate AI, which is one of the strongest parts of the platform. The role of AI here is not novelty. It is guided decision support. For an employee, it helps identify the most relevant pathway and supports the application journey. For managers, it supports team development thinking. For department heads, it supports workforce insight. For apprenticeship leads, it supports role-to-standard mapping and provider matching."

"The reason this matters is that most people do not want to navigate multiple sections to find the right answer. They want a guided route in."

"Over time, this becomes the natural front door to the platform."

What value it demonstrates:

- simpler navigation
- faster pathway discovery
- better decision support
- role-specific guidance
- stronger product differentiation

### 4. Manager Workflow

What to say:

"From the Line Manager perspective, LevyTate makes approvals more structured and more credible. Managers can see requests from direct reports, understand the context, review the business case and make a decision without relying on back-and-forth email."

"This is important because manager confidence is one of the biggest practical barriers to apprenticeship adoption. If managers do not understand the value, demand slows down. LevyTate gives them the context they need to make better decisions."

What value it demonstrates:

- cleaner approval flow
- better manager engagement
- faster decisions
- better development planning at team level
- less admin friction

### 5. Department Head Analytics

What to say:

"Department Heads are not here to approve applications. Their role is to understand whether development activity is improving capability. So this view is about analytics: participation, skills gaps, site-level visibility, future demand and workforce readiness."

"This is where LevyTate moves beyond apprenticeship administration and starts to look like a real workforce planning tool."

What value it demonstrates:

- leadership-grade analytics
- department-level planning
- site and skills visibility
- clearer link between apprenticeships and business readiness

### 6. Apprenticeship Lead Controls

What to say:

"For Apprenticeship Leads, this becomes the operating centre. They can manage pathways, review applications that have already passed manager approval, oversee approved-for-enrolment activity, track provider mappings and run reporting."

"This is one of the most important parts of the platform commercially and operationally because it gives the employer a single place to manage apprenticeship demand at scale."

What value it demonstrates:

- single operating environment
- cleaner internal controls
- stronger governance
- improved levy and programme oversight
- reduced manual coordination

### 7. Provider Matching

What to say:

"This is where LevyTate becomes commercially distinct. Most platforms stop at applications or reporting. LevyTate goes one step further and helps the employer move from need to provider support. Apprenticeship Leads can request provider matching based on workforce need, learner volume, site footprint and delivery preference."

"That means LevyTate is not just showing data. It is helping the employer make the next operational decision."

"It also gives the LevyTate team a clear route into consultancy, advisory and provider support conversations."

What value it demonstrates:

- easier provider decision-making
- clearer next step after demand is identified
- a stronger commercial model
- better employer confidence
- differentiates LevyTate from marketplaces and LMS tools

## Section 5

## Objection Handling

### "We already have providers"

Recommended response:

"That is completely fine. LevyTate is not trying to replace good provider relationships. It helps you understand where your existing providers fit well, where gaps exist, and where you may need structured matching support. In many cases, LevyTate strengthens the value you get from providers you already trust."

### "We already use apprenticeships"

Recommended response:

"That usually means you already have activity. It does not always mean you have a scalable operating model. LevyTate is valuable because it helps turn apprenticeship usage into something more strategic, more visible and easier to manage across employees, managers, sites and leadership."

### "We already have an LMS"

Recommended response:

"That is helpful, but an LMS solves a different problem. An LMS manages learning content and completion. LevyTate manages apprenticeship demand, pathway fit, approvals, provider planning and workforce readiness. It sits upstream of delivery rather than replacing learning systems."

### "We don't have levy"

Recommended response:

"LevyTate is still relevant because the challenge is bigger than levy alone. Employers still need clearer pathways, stronger approvals, better workforce planning and better provider decisions. LevyTate helps structure demand and identify the right development routes even where levy funding is limited or unavailable."

### "We don't have enough people"

Recommended response:

"That is often exactly why employers need a better system. When resource is tight, fragmented admin becomes even more expensive. LevyTate helps reduce manual coordination, guide people into the right routes faster, and give leaders a clearer picture without creating more process overhead."
